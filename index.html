How we work

A four-step prove-before-scale process.

Every engagement starts with a real diagnostic on your book. Nothing scales until it beats the incumbent process in measured lift.

①
01 — Diagnose

Diagnose

Two weeks inside your underwriting, claims and data teams. We map the workflow, the friction points, and where AI actually moves the needle versus where it's noise.

②
02 — Design

Design

We scope the smallest valuable build — model, integration and governance. You see the success metric, the rollback plan and the regulatory posture before anyone writes code.

③
03 — Validate

Validate

We backtest against your real submissions, claims or policies. Lift measured against the incumbent process — challenger versus champion — with full auditability.

④
04 — Scale

Scale

Production deployment, monitoring, drift detection and a handover that survives staff turnover. We stay until your team owns the model end-to-end.
